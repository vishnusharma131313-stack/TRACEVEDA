About TraceVeda

A clearer picture of every medicine’s journey.

TraceVeda brings supply-chain activity and supporting evidence into one connected view — from source and production to quality checks, storage and delivery.

It is designed to help participating organisations record important events, review product history and make verification easier for the people who handle or receive a medicine.

01
Connected records

Keep key batch events and documents linked across the journey.

02
Evidence at each stage

Bring lab results, movement updates and monitoring data together.

03
Clearer verification

Make it easier to review a batch history and check a medicine QR.

How it works

One journey. Connected checkpoints.

Each participant adds or reviews the evidence relevant to their stage.

01 / SOURCE

Register the batch

Record the source and create a traceable batch identity.

02 / QUALITY

Verify quality

Laboratories review the batch and record their test outcome.

03 / MOVEMENT

Monitor handling

Follow transport and storage conditions with IoT evidence.

04 / VERIFICATION

Check the medicine

Use the QR journey to review the available product history.

A few quick answers

Good to know.

Useful context before you enter the platform.

What can I check with a medicine QR?

You can open the available batch information and review the traceability details recorded for that medicine.

Who can use TraceVeda?

Approved manufacturers, laboratories, transporters and auditors can access role-specific workspaces. Consumers can use the public verification flow.

What does IoT monitoring add?

It brings sensor readings from supported transport and storage checkpoints into the batch evidence trail.
